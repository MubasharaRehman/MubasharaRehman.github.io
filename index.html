ICIAP · 2025

ReMAR-DS: Metal Artifact Reduction and Domain Transformation from kVCT to MVCT

Mubashara Rehman, Niki Martinel, Michele Avanzo, Riccardo Spizzo, Christian Micheloni

Metal artifacts in kVCT can affect image quality and clinical interpretation. We propose ReMAR-DS, an encoder–decoder framework for metal artifact reduction and kVCT-to-MVCT domain transformation. The method emphasizes artifact-prone regions while preserving anatomical structures to generate high-quality MVCT-like reconstructions for radiotherapy applications.

ACM SIGIR Forum · 2025

Report on the Hands-On PhD Course on Responsible AI from the Lens of an Information Access Researcher

Damiano Spina, Kevin Roitero, Stefano Mizzaro, Vincenzo Della Mea, Francesca Da Ros, Michael Soprano, Hafsa Akebli, Alex Falcon, Mehdi Fasihi, Alessio Fiorin, David La Barbera, Daniele Lizzio Bosco, Riccardo Lunardi, Alberto Marturano, Zaka-Ud-Din Muhammad, Francesco Nascimben, Moritz Nottebaum, Massimiliano Pascoli, Mihai Horia Popescu, Laura Rasotto, Mubashara Rehman, Francesco Taverna, Biagio Tomasetig, Alessandro Tremamunno

This report presents a four-day PhD-level course on Responsible Artificial Intelligence held at the University of Udine. The course explored topics including positionality, fairness, diversity, ethics and participatory research through case studies and interactive activities.

ICPR · 2024

MAR-DTN: Metal Artifact Reduction Using Domain Transformation Network for Radiotherapy Planning

Belén Serrano-Antón, Mubashara Rehman, Niki Martinel, Michele Avanzo, Riccardo Spizzo, Giuseppe Fanetti, Alberto P. Muñuzuri, Christian Micheloni

We propose a UNet-inspired domain transformation network for generating artifact-free MVCT images from kVCT inputs. The approach combines information from both modalities to reduce metallic artifacts while improving tissue representation for radiotherapy treatment planning.

ICSCA · 2021

A Fast and Robust Embedded Optical Flow Unit on Motion Estimation

Mubashara Rehman, Chung Ming Own, Ziqi Li

We propose a fast and robust optical flow estimation unit that enhances FlowNetC using correlation-based feature matching, an Extended Sobel operator and feature-fusion strategies. Experiments on MPI Sintel and Flying Chairs demonstrate competitive accuracy, convergence and robustness.
