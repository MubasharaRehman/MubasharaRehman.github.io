Mubashara Rehman

AI researcher working at the intersection of deep learning, computer vision, medical imaging, and generative AI.

I recently submitted my PhD thesis in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Udine , Italy. My doctoral research focused on AI-driven medical image analysis, with particular emphasis on metal artifact reduction, CT reconstruction, and cross-modality domain transformation for radiotherapy applications.
